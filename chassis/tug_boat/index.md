
## Tug Boat

Boat chassis provides several advantages over other vehicle forms:
- Simple control - thrust and rudder
- Simplified navigation - no terrain, no restricted flying zones
- High payload capacity - marine has a comparable high capacity per size and power use which allows more versatility with electronics and components

## Design

- Original design: https://www.printables.com/model/225276-rc-boat-134-parts-1907-tug-from-damen
- Scaled at 84% (hull size - 503 mm)
- Converted into a single-prop config


## Materials

- 3D printed with PETG plastic on Bambu Lab A1 mini
- Hull had to be split onto 6 pieces to fit the print bed
- Localized reinforcement with fiberglass composite


## Components

- Motor - SSS 650kv, 540 body type
- ESC - ZTW Shark, 20A 
- Propeller - 40 mm
- Servo - DS-S007M, 21g
- Receiver - FlySky FS-iA6B
- Battery - 2S
